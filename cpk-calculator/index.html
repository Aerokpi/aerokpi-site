02Worked example

Shaft diameter, 10.00 ± 0.05 mm. LSL = 9.95, USL = 10.05, mean = 10.012, σ = 0.012.

  • Cp = 0.10 / (6 × 0.012) = 1.39
  • Cpu = (10.05 − 10.012) / 0.036 = 1.06; Cpl = (10.012 − 9.95) / 0.036 = 1.72
  • Cpk = 1.06, about 770 PPM above the USL

The spread would pass 1.33, but the process is running 0.012 mm high. Re-centering it lifts Cpk to 1.39 without touching variation.

03How it works

Cp compares the width of the tolerance to the width of the process (six standard deviations). It ignores where the process is centered, so it tells you what you could achieve if you were perfectly on target.

Cpk measures the distance from the mean to the nearest spec limit, in units of three standard deviations. If Cpk is much lower than Cp, the process is off center. Move the mean before you spend money on reducing variation.

Cp = (USL - LSL) / (6 × σwithin) Cpu = (USL - mean) / (3 × σwithin) Cpl = (mean - LSL) / (3 × σwithin) Cpk = min(Cpu, Cpl) Pp, Ppk: same formulas using σoverall

With raw data, σ within is estimated from the average moving range divided by d2 = 1.128, which is the standard method for individual readings. σ overall is the ordinary sample standard deviation. That is why the data needs to stay in production order. Expected PPM assumes a normal distribution.

Common targets

  • 1.33 is the usual minimum for production characteristics.
  • 1.67 is common for safety or key characteristics and for new processes (PPAP).
  • Always confirm the target in your customer's requirements.

04FAQ

What is a good Cpk value?

Most customers want at least 1.33 for production characteristics, and 1.67 for safety or key characteristics or during initial approval (PPAP). Below 1.00 means the process is producing parts outside the limits.

What is the difference between Cp and Cpk?

Cp only compares the spread of the process to the tolerance width. Cpk also accounts for where the process is centered. A process can have a high Cp and a low Cpk if it is off target.

What is the difference between Cpk and Ppk?

Cpk uses within (short-term) variation, estimated here from the moving range. Ppk uses overall (long-term) variation, the plain standard deviation of all readings. A big gap between them means the process shifts or drifts over time.

How many measurements do I need?

Use at least 25 to 30 readings, and 100 or more for a reliable capability study. Small samples give unstable Cpk values. Make sure the process is in statistical control before you trust the result.

Can I use this with a one-sided tolerance?

Yes. Leave LSL or USL blank. Cp can't be calculated without both limits, so the calculator reports Cpk as Cpu or Cpl.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The calculation runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
