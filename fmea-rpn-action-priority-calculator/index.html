    03How it works

    RPN (risk priority number) is severity × occurrence × detection, from 1 to 1,000. It is simple, but very different risks can share the same number, and a severity 10 failure can rank below a frequent nuisance.

    RPN = S × O × D AP = lookup in the AIAG-VDA table by S band, then O band, then D band

    Action Priority comes from the 2019 AIAG-VDA FMEA Handbook. Instead of multiplying, it looks up the combination in a table that weights severity first, then occurrence, then detection. High means action is required or the team must justify why not. Medium means action should be taken. Low means action could be taken.

    The table on the left shows the AP lookup for the severity band of the row you are editing, with your rating highlighted. The lookup used here is the process FMEA (PFMEA) table. Your customer's handbook edition and any customer-specific rules take precedence.

    04FAQ

    What is the difference between RPN and Action Priority?

    RPN multiplies the three ratings, so it treats severity, occurrence and detection as equally important. Action Priority uses a lookup table that weights severity most, then occurrence, then detection, so high-severity risks can't be hidden by a good detection score.

    Is RPN still allowed?

    Many customers still accept RPN, but the AIAG-VDA handbook (2019) replaced it with Action Priority, and many automotive and aerospace customers now expect AP. Check your customer's requirements.

    Is there an RPN threshold that requires action?

    The AIAG FMEA manuals discourage fixed RPN thresholds such as acting on anything over 100. With AP, every High item needs action or a documented justification.

    Does this use the DFMEA or the PFMEA table?

    It uses the PFMEA Action Priority table from the AIAG-VDA FMEA Handbook, first edition (2019). The DFMEA table uses the same structure, but the rating criteria behind S, O and D differ.

    Why does a severity 9 or 10 item with occurrence 1 come out Low?

    In the AIAG-VDA table, occurrence 1 means the failure is practically prevented, so AP is Low for any severity. Many teams still review every severity 9 and 10 item separately, and some customers require it.

    Is my data saved?

    No. Rows stay in your browser tab and disappear when you close it.
